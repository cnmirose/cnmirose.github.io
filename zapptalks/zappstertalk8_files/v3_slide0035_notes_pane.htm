Let’s take another walk down memory lane.  In the 1980s, I bought a PC with a really fast modem-- about 10,000 bits per second.  Email seemed instantaneous, and I could send that PowerPoint file in 5 minutes-- kind of slow but it was acceptable.
Today I can get DSL, of course, and get a million bits/second-- for $40/month.   I can download that Powerpoint file in 3 seconds-- but it would still take me over an hour to download that music CD or VCR quality movie, and 11 hours to download that DVD movie.
The DVD is clearly not reasonable, but before we move on, let’s take a closer look at that VCR quality movie.  We seem to be able to deliver it in less time than it takes to watch it, so we could “stream” it to the home and watch it while it’s arriving.  This is a real possibility that people are experimenting with to deliver video on demand, so it deserves mention.  It’s a viable approach, but there are a number of problems ...
