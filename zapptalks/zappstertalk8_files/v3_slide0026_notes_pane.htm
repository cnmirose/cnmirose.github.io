We need to start by asking what a giant file is, and why it’s hard to deliver.  One problem in answering that is that the answer keeps changing.  Not long ago, it was a PowerPoint presentation with ClipArt-- maybe 400 KiloBytes.  Then there were Music CD’s that contained 400 MegaBytes.  Now there are DVD movies that contain 4 GigaBytes.
Most people don’t appreciate the huge difference in size between what we used to want to send, and what we now want to send.  One reason is that the terms change-- from Kilo to Mega to Giga, so the numbers don’t get bigger.  We went from 400 to 400 to 4 “something or others”.  Another reason is that the physical packages look the same-- the floppy disk that holds my PowerPoint, the music CD and the movie DVD look about the same size.  A third reason is that we now believe the Internet can do anything, so why worry about how big the file is.
Let’s try to get a feel for the relative sizes of these three files.
