It gets even Better
•Encryption- solves the security problem
•Download multiple selections
•Pay only for what you watch?
•Nothing to return
•Personalized advertising
• Previews can be ordered, or customized for the individual
The fact that we are downloading a digital file to a known customer has some very significant advantages.  Unlike DVDs, we can encrypt each download individually, and provide a key to the PDP that can’t be shared.
We can monitor and control the number of viewings, so we can even allow you to bring home several movies, and give you credit for those you don’t watch.
There is nothing to return, so you save the second trip, and don’t have to watch it the same day.
And because the download is “personal”, we can provide you with personalized advertising.  This might mean that you can order preview clips of movies that interest you, or that we could provide such clips based on your “profile”, eliminating those you have seen and providing new ones that are likely to be of interest based on your viewing history.  As you are probably thinking, this also raises the possibility of personalized advertising that goes beyond film clips– but more on that later.
It’s time to expand our horizon– in two dimensions.
