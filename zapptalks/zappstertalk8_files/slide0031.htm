That takes us through the basic description of Zappster.  There are a number of other topics that we have looked at and decided not to discuss in detail today.  For example, we have technology and cost trends for magnetic storage.  We’ve taken a back of the envelope look at system costs, and how we might collect the revenues– we favor an EasyPass type of debit system.  We’ve looked at file security.  And we’re working on some demos.  For example we can demo the ordering process at our website, the download at the kiosk, and playing the content at home or on the road.  We can illustrate PDPs like this that play the content through TVs or laptops, and more elegant ones like this that let you order and even play the content directly.  All these demos are created out of existing pieceparts, of course, but they show that the basic technology is available for a rich set of services.
