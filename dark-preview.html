Darker, and quieter

No dot field. No rail running down the left. Depth now comes from two very large, very soft colour blooms — no repeating pattern, nothing for the eye to catch on. Sections are separated by space and by a short accent bar above the heading, instead of ruled lines.

The link lists have also lost their per-row rules. That was the "ledger" feeling, I think: too many horizontal lines at even intervals.
