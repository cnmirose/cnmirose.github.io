The technology is a surprising one-- the magnetic hard disk such as we have in every PC and laptop.  It’s an amazing technology that has a better learning curve than the famous Moore’s law for chips.  It’s capacity has doubled every year for more than a decade, while it has grown smaller and faster and cheaper.  It’s a tremendously competitive market-- companies have names like “dirt cheap drives,” and advertise “blowout sales”.  Within a couple of years, a hard disk that fits in you shirt pocket will hold several DVD movies and cost less than $100.  You will be able to load a DVD into it in 15-30 seconds.
That same technology will be available in larger packages having many disks, that will cost only a few thousand dollars and store thousands of movies-- and every year this technology will keep getting better.  The question was how to build an exciting system and service out of this technology.  The answer is that we use these storage devices not only to to store the giant files, but to move them around as well.  This physical movement of the content is much faster and much cheaper than the wireline and radio alternatives.  It allows us to offer a virtually unlimited menu of content to the customer, at very low cost.
