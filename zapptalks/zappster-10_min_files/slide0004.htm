Service Center
The Zappster hybrid network includes a service center, where a really huge amount of content is stored.  Special orders arrive at the service center website, and are automatically scheduled and confirmed for deliver at a particular kiosk at a particular time.  For example, special orders placed by early afternoon could be available for pickup by the evening commute. The special order content is then downloaded to transportable disk packs, which are physically delivered to the kiosks on a regular scheduled route.  In addition, DSL lines to the kiosks can be used for Internet deliver of smaller files, such as stock quotes and personal messages.
The capacity of this physical delivery channel is so large that it is virtually infinite when compared to DSL or wireless options, and the cost is essentially independent of the amount delivered.  In addition to the special orders, this physical delivery network will handle updates of the kiosk menu, including new movies and network programs.
