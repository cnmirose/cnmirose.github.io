FINER POINTS:
Then there's the issue of how to search for what you want and specify it.  That's sometimes trickier.  For example for something like "RFID tag" you need to narrow it down and see if you can find a part number.  You may even need to get creative -- for instance, RFID tags show up also under "inventory control systems" -- just as you do when you google for that thing you've seen out there but can't quite name.

Other things are completely generic, like all sorts of standard cabling (ethernet, usb, etc.)

However, the bottom line is tat you can't what you want and specify it on the "approved vendor" list then something's wrong.

SO:  please go back to your parts lists and locate what you need at the above vendors.  Then prepare a list with the vendor and part number (and price) number of units, etc. and SEND IT TO ME for pre-James-vetting. 

WHEN YOU ABSOLUTELY POSITIVELY NEED A NEW VENDOR:

The process for adding a vendor approved is as follow:

  • 1.       Determine if the vendor accepts PO’s and Standard Net30 Terms (if they don’t, we’re done)
  • 2.       If they do, get them to fill out a new supplier form
  • 3.       Specfiy to the vendor that the form must be submitted (probably with a w9) to procure@rci.rutgers.edu (instructions are on the form)
  • 4.       Wait for purchasing to complete the processing of the vendor submission (2-5 days).

Many things often go wrong, so what one typically does is submit the completed from oneself (have the vendor send the form directly to you). That way Rutgers Purchasing has a local contact.  (James is not sure if a person with RIAS access has to do this submission  -- when not submitted by the vendor itself. ) In either case you should ask for a copy of the complete form from the vendor as well in case purchasing has some issues, you’ll have something to refer to.

That being said, WINLAB Staff (James in particular) can handle steps 3-4 for you. Steps 1 and 2 usually involve some kind of phone leg work, and a sales contact at the vendor. If they get those done it shortens the process.  Note that the RIAS database is a shared resource and as such is very easily polluted.  DO NOT attempt to add new vendors before doing due dilengence (carefully checking the existing list)
