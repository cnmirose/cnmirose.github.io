Interference
Avoidance

Convergence via class warfare

Radios that iteratively adapt their own codewords — greedily, selfishly, with no central authority — and still converge to a jointly optimal allocation. It works, it converges, and the convergence argument goes by the name class warfare.

2003 IEEE Marconi Prize Paper Award

Wireless Systems and Interference Avoidance

C. Rose, S. Ulukus and R. Yates, IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications 1(3), p. 415, July 2002.
