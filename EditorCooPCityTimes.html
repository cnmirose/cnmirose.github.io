To The Editor:


When a woman is raped the rapist’s attorney blames the victim. He tells the jury, “she asked for it”. The MTA, by gerrymandering our bus routes, has raped every single one of us in Co-op City! Our representatives having betrayed us by not providing leadership defend their betrayal by telling the jury, “they asked for it”. And, adding insult to injury, exclaimed that we in Co-op City were apathetic and therefore compliant.


How dare you? Even the least sophisticated among us knows that the travesty manifest in MTA’s highhanded decision didn’t happen overnight. It was more than a year in formulation and by the time the termites revealed their presence the success of an effective uprising was rendered hopeless. And Public Hearings became a mere sham!

Where were you when we needed you, Representative Crowley, Assemblyman Benedetto, and Councilman Seabrook? State Senator, Hassell-Thompson? Those so-called civic activists in our midst? The Board of Directors? Our Building Association presidents?


They knew or should have known what was being perpetrated against us. So, collectively, they share the responsibility for allowing the MTA to commit this violation against an aging, innocent community. It was their lack of leadership not the ineffectual defense put forth by inference that it was our apathy that created this inexplicably horrendous inconvenience.


After the fact, one of those toothless tigers proclaimed that these outrageous bus routes are now “written in stone”? No they’re not! If our representatives have any decency or moral fiber or, more to the point, want to earn their re-election, their first order of business must be to threaten a lawsuit against the MTA on our behalf. Or, at a minimum, put extreme political pressure on the MTA to reverse this cruel and defenseless decision which benefits no one.

The residents of Co-op City didn’t “ask” for this. We don’t deserve this. And, who told you we had to suffer this indignity without protest? The MTA’s decision while intolerable is not immutable. Those currently in power have a moral obligation to prevail upon the MTA to reverse this ill-conceived decision which, at best, can only be described as a spiteful and punitive act upon a helpless population.


Yours truly,


Jeanne J. Rose
