The answer was once again to recognize that original lesson.  Our customer carrying the loaded PDP home represents a massive delivery rate-- and we can use that same technique of physical delivery to bring much greater amounts of content from the service center to the kiosk.  Instead of a PDP, we load the content into a cluster of disks similar to what we have described for the kiosk, and physically transport that device to the kiosks on a scheduled daily route.  At each kiosk, the delivery person will use a high speed connection to upload the new content into the kiosk.
The capacity of this physical delivery channel is so large that it is virtually infinite when compared to Internet connections, and the cost is essentially independent of the amount delivered.  In addition to the special orders, this physical delivery network will handle updates of the kiosk menu, including new movies and network programs.  Special orders placed by early afternoon will be available for pickup by the evening commute.
We can use our own personnel to deliver the content, or contract with a commercial service like UPS, which would probably be cheaper and may be faster as well.
