David Evans eloquently summarizes his 40 years of admissions at the Harvard Black Graduation '09. The tree was shaken and the falling fruit has been picked up by literally thousands of Black Harvard graduates. Dave's call? As with every generation, we were beneficiaries of others' efforts. Thus, ONCE PICKERS, NOW SHAKERS WE MUST BE for those who come behind us -- especially the larger and ignored portion of our diaspora. Here's what Dave ACTUALLY said: EDUCATION, LIKE FREEDOM, HAS NEVER BEEN FREE. WHEN WE PICKED UP PEACHES, SOMEONE ELSE SHOOK THE TREE. I WAS A PICKER; NOW MAY I A SHAKER BE. AS I HAVE GAINED, LET SOMEONE ELSE NOW GAIN FROM ME.
