Chatroulette:  Chatroulette is a social website on which users from around the world are instantly connected to one another through their webcams. Once a person logs onto the site, his/her computer screen will be split; one half will show the user live, and the other will show a random person who happens to be logged on at the same time. If a user doesn’t like what he/she sees (i.e. the other user they have been connected to), he or she simply clicks the mouse and is taken to the next person. Similarly, if the user to whom a person is connected to doesn’t like what he/she sees, he/she can move on to the next person in line, leaving the user to wonder why he/she was passed on.
The issues with Chatroulette are numerous, beginning with the fact that there is no minimum age required to use the site, no fees attached, no email address needed, and it lacks the obviously sexual name that would indicate to a parent/adult that there may be inappropriate behavior on it for young people.
Most people who know about the site (including some of our students) say that its current use is mostly sexual in nature. Chatroulette has quickly become filled with people exposing body parts, masturbating, and looking to entice sexual behavior from their viewer. Ernie Allen, president of the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children called what's happening on Chatroulette, "…a huge red flag. This is extreme social networking. Absolutely random. No limits. Graphic sexual content. This is the last place parents want their kids to be." "ttp://www.cbsnews.com/stories/2010/02/23/earlyshow/leisure/gamesgadgetsgizmos/main6234749.shtml
With celebrities like Paris Hilton, Jessica Alba, and Aston Kutcher endorsing the use of this site, the enormity of the problem (kids seeing pornographic images, kids being asked to perform sexual acts on camera, screen shots of young people being taken and used elsewhere, the possibility for child predators to entice young people to share personal information with them, etc.) is only increased.  For a young person innocently mimicking one of their favorite stars, using this site can seem harmless.  Yet, to those in charge of the safety and well being of young people, it is very scary.                                                                                                            
The Dangers of Chatroulette for Kids                                                                                                                     
Inappropriate Content. The most obvious danger for kids would be the inappropriate content. There is nothing that penalizes users for doing obscene things in their chat room, especially because they can’t get kicked off or banned – there is no sign up. The fact that you cannot filter whom you might see creates a danger for kids because they might end up chatting with someone older, someone who is looking specifically to influence young children. They can see them clearly on the web cam and might try to get them to say or do things that are inappropriate.

Ability to Record Chats & Video. In this day and age, most everything you do on the Internet can be recorded and used in perpetuity for all to see and Chatroulette is no different. Texts and chats can be recorded and put up on other Internet sites – YouTube is a popular place for funny Chatroulette videos, but it isn’t so funny if your child gets caught doing something stupid or obscene and they are all of the sudden Internet stars.
Rejection. Some kids might not understand the process of being “nexted” or rejected by the person on the other side of the chat, especially teenagers who are self conscious about their looks. Even if it is someone from across the globe, teenagers and younger kids can still wrap up their self-identity in other people.
