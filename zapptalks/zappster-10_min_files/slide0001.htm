At WINLAB, we’ve spent a number of years working on the problem  of delivering giant files through radio systems.  When we say “giant”,  we’re talking about files a thousand times bigger than the largest email attachment you would ever try to send today-- files like a DVD movie, for example.  Even with the most optimistic assumptions for the next generation of wireless systems, such a download would take 10-30 hours and cost a ridiculous amount.   And it isn’t just wireless.  Using DSL to your home, it would still take 10-20 hours to download a DVD movie.
Others are taking the route of using lower quality formats to deliver movies to the home over DSL, but we believe that with large screens TVs becoming so popular, and with high definition coming, quality will become increasingly important.  In thinking about it, we realized that there was a technology and method that could allow us to deliver a variety of giant files, including not only movies but TV programming, music, software and local information as well, and that we could simultaneously serve the home and mobile markets.
