 Registration Notes

       Conference registration includes one copy of the conference proceedings, the Chairman's Welcome Reception on Monday, admittance to all papers and panel sessions; lunches and refreshment breaks on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday; One ticket to the Conference Banquet on Thursday; One ticket to the Concert in the Pantheon, and one ticket to the concert <NAME IS MISSING>. It does not include Tutorials or Workshops. Additional tickets for the Conference Banquet, the Concerts, and additional copies of the Conference Proceedings are available for additional cost.

      Workshop registration for each workshop includes one copy of the workshop proceedings, admittance to all sessions of the workshop you have registered for, one lunch ticket, and refreshment breaks. Additional Workshop Proceedings may be purchased for additional cost. Conference Proceedings, conference admittance, tutorial notes and tutorial admittance are not included with workshop registration.

      Tutorial registration includes a copy of the tutorial notes for the tutorial you have registered for and refreshment breaks. A lunch ticket is included with a full-day tutorial registration and/or if you register for two half-day tutorials on the same day. You must pay for each tutorial you register for. Conference proceedings and workshop proceedings are not included with tutorial registration.

      Early registration deadline is June 1, 2001. In order to be on the attendee list and receive a preprinted badge you must register by July 12, 2001. On-line registration ends on July 12, 2001. After that you must register on-site.

      Discounted prices are available to members of ACM, ACM SIGMOBILE, ACM SIGOPS, ACM SIGCOMM, ACM SIGMETRICS, ACM SIGMOD. These prices are also available to IEEE Communication Society members provided you give us a verifiable membership number with your registration.  If you are currently not a member, you should consider joining, getting your membership number, and then registering to get the discount prices.

     Student registration fees require a valid student ID to be shown at the conference.

      Written requests for refunds must be postmarked no later than July 1, 2001. Refunds are subject to a US $75 service charge. Participants with confirmed registration who fail to attend or notify MobiCom registration of cancellation before the refund date are subject to the full fee.
