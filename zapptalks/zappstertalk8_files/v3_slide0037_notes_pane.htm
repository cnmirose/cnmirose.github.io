Specifically, we’ve looked at using that high speed Infostation radio for the download from kiosk to PDP, so the customer could be a hundred feet from the kiosk, in a car (maybe even moving).  That would be convenient and add security in some locations.  Radio would also allow multiple access, without plugging in, and access in new types of location, like gas stations and toll booths.
Our research in this area involves both radio design and protocols.  In fact we have significant federal and state grants as the creator and lead institution in this technology.  We think that a practical, low-cost radio can achieve short range download rates almost as high as high as by plugging in.  Still, the radio option is at least 5 years off, and we don’t see it as necessary to the basic concept of Zappster.
