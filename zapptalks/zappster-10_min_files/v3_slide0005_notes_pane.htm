In summary, we have discussed the problem of delivering giant files and proposed a new method.
We have shown that while video rental is a effective technique, Zappster offers powerful advantages.  It is cheaper, better serves a mobile population, and can deliver many new forms of content.
We have shown how magnetic storage technology is the key to a large menu of content in the kiosk, an almost infinite menu on special order through a hybrid network, and a small, low-cost PDP.
We think the costs look attractive.
Looking out a few more years, we think that Zappster can evolve into a dramatic new type of 2-way global network for giant files.  We can achieve this by interconnecting our service centers through low-cost, high-density fiber routes, and using our local hybrid network to complete the delivery at low cost, where fiber isn’t available.
We can even extend our network beyond the kiosk, and deliver and pick up files at the home.
What is particularly exciting is that this is a proven technology and the initial market is pretty well understood.  Thus we can get started quickly, with little risk, based on existing technology and markets, which evolving gradually into a more complete global network
