Zappster Global Network
And finally, we come to the last step in our evolution– Zappster as a global network.  We have noted that we can’t economically deliver GigaBytes to the places people want them over landlines, but we know that where the broadband fiber network exists it had incredible capacity, and low cost.  By placing our service centers on this network, we can move our bits anywhere in the world for pennies a Gigabyte, and then deliver them with our hybrid network.  An we can do that in both directions– our customers can bring their giant files to a kiosk for pickup, and within a few hours they can be at any other kiosk in the country, or the world.  We can also offer different grades of service.  For a higher price, we can deliver files to the home– perhaps even using the US postal service.  We can deliver them on the regular schedule, or rush a special order to the customer as soon as it arrives.
The implications of this are significant.  We have customers carrying a large amount of information in a small package.  It could include all the information in their PC, allowing them to use any computer they find rather than carrying one everywhere.  In fact, one might carry all the text one has ever read, accessed through smart data base retrieval software.  Using Zappster, the content of that disk can be backed up on the network, and delivered to the customer anywhere.  Software vendors could install new software for you.
Our Blockbuster in a box has evolved to an international network that can deliver any size file, anywhere, at very low cost.
