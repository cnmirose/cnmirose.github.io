Zappster Frequently Asked Questions

Team Zappster

General Questions  

  • What Is Zappster? Zappster is a method for inexpensive delivery of large personal files such as DVD movies, audio tapes, software, recorded network television programs (including reruns), family videos, snapshots or any other digital information ("content") you desire.

  • What Are The Components Of Zappster? The principal components of Zappster are the Personal Data Packs, the Kiosks, the Service Center and the Hybrid Distribution Network.
    • What Is A Personal Data Pack?  Personal Data Packs are easily portable hand-held mass storage devices into which content can be rapidly loaded and later used/played back through either an external or an integral player. Content on a Personal Data Pack can also be uploaded at high speed to a Zappster Kiosk.

      • How Much Will My Personal Data Pack Hold? Today, a low cost personal data pack with 6 GB storage could hold 100 hours of MPEG3 audio, 8 audio CDs of VHS-quality movies and one DVD movie.

      • What Does A Personal Data Pack Cost? Small 20 GB disks are available today for less than $100 retail. We expect the Personal Data Pack cost to be near this price.

    • What Is A Zappster Kiosk?  The Kiosk is a mass storage unit which holds the content to be downloaded to the Personal Data Pack at high speed. Heavily used content such as popular movies and audio are stored at each Kiosk. Special orders are delivered to the Kiosk as needed. Kiosks also accept uploads from Personal Data Packs and can be networked to the Internet as well.

    • What Is The Zappster Service Center?  The Service Center is the central point of contact for the customer and also controls network functions. User orders are received at the service center, accounts credited and debited, encryption keys supplied and transport for special orders arranged.

    • What Is The Hybrid Distribution Network?  The Hybrid Distribution Network is a combination of wireline/wireless and physical transport of content. Content which does not change rapidly over time (SUCH AS movies, audio, software and UNLIKE news) can be copied from a large central repository, and then physically transported and stored in Kiosks by service personnel via rapid upload or by adding a storage unit to the Kiosk. Content can also be delivered via wireline or satellite links as well depending on the amount and the deadline by which the content must be available. However, the primary method of large content delivery to Kiosks will be via physical transport. This is what makes Zappster easy to deploy and maintain at low cost per download.

  • How Is Content Downloaded? The user carries a Personal Data Pack to (or near) a convenient Kiosk. Content is then downloaded either through plugging the Personal Data Pack into a port on the Kiosk or wirelessly over a short distance (< 50 yards).

  • How Long Does It Take To Download Content? After the content is available at the Kiosk, for both plug-in and short range wireless links, a typical DVD movie download would require 40 seconds. An audio CD would require 6 seconds. 60 minutes of MPEG3 encoded audio would require less than 1/2 second. This incredibly fast transfer of content is one of the most powerful features of Zappster.

  • But How Do I Actually Use Zappster? You make an order for content by browsing the Zappster site on the Internet, browsing in person at a Zappster Kiosk, or via a phone call to a Zappster Service Center . You are charged either by one-time credit card use or through a user account tied to a credit card. You and the system agree on the location of the Kiosk you will use for download and the time by which the content will be available. You then plug your Personal Data Pack into that Kiosk at or after the agreed upon time and rapidly obtain your order. In the future short range (< 50 yards) wireless transfer will also be possible. You then attach your Personal Data Pack to an external device such as a television/vcr/dvd player a PC or PDA to play the content. A more expensive version of the Personal Data Pack could have built-in players.

  • Can I Use Zappster Without Leaving My Home?  With wireless links, the Zappster Hybrid Distribution Network could potentially be used to deliver content in a "take out" fashion directly to the home by personnel already employed to service Kiosks.

  • Can I Always Get My Content Right Away? If the content is resident at a nearby Kiosk then you can obtain your content as soon as you arrive at the Kiosk. If the content is not resident, then it must be delivered and the delivery will be made by an agreed upon deadline. A typical scenario might be an order placed during the afternoon from work and content pickup at a Kiosk during the homeward commute - for instance, at a mass transportation terminal or toll plaza. Likewise, one might place an order from home and the content might be delivered wirelessly during a drive-by.

  • How Do I Pay For Zappster Content? Your Zappster content is debited from your credit card either as a one-time use or through an account.

  • Can I Use Zappster When I'm On The Move? YES! One of the features of Zappster is that orders can be placed anywhere and so long as there is a Kiosk convenient to your location, you can obtain the content you order. With a mobile player, you can also enjoy the content when you are on the move.

  • Can I Use Zappster With Different Playback Equipment? YES! The Personal Data Pack is easily transportable and contains all the necessary equipment to convert the content into a form suitable for external players such as TVs, VCRs, Stereos, PCs, PDAs and more. For instance, the Personal Data Pack could be used with any TV in the house, or any PC with a suitable player.

  • What Is The Difference Between Zappster And Traditional Video Rental?  Video tapes must be picked up and then returned to the store, often after suffering a late fee. Multiple but limited copies of each title must be kept on hand by the store and displayed for easy perusal. Customers are often frustrated to find the the movie they want is not available. Special items such as the previous evening's news, or re-runs of a favorite program are not economical to produce and stock since they would be custom items. Personnel must be hired to maintain the store and process transactions with customers and sufficient floor space must be obtained to house the enterprise.

    In contrast, the Zappster Kiosk can produce essentially unlimited copies of any title it contains, has a small, easily securable footprint, requires no dedicated personnel and can be loaded with special order items from a central repository as needed. Zappster opens up new frontiers in video rental by making economical the sale of a much wider range of entertainment than possible under the traditional video rental paradigm. Furthermore, the content can be encrypted with a one time or multiple use key so that content viewing is limited without requiring a rental return.

  • What Is The Difference Between Zappster And Video-On-Demand?  Most current video on demand systems force users to experience their entertainment at preset times. Furthermore, the palette of choices is often limited and the user has no control over the flow of content after it has been started. Recently proposed systems intend to use DSL connections to the home to deliver VHS-quality real-time video streams under user control - just as one would experience using a VCR. Such systems are convenient in that the user need not leave home and does not need to wait for content delivery. However, such systems require setting up a DSL link to the home and not all homes are DSL capable. Furthermore, the incoming connection cannot be easily connected to different TVs without some sort of in-home networking. Such systems also do not allow user mobility and viewing "on the move" with laptops, portable TVs or PDAs. The quality of video-on-demand is limited whereas Zappster can provide a full range of quality from PDA to DVD. Finally, it is hard to understand how a network based on massive wireline access and associated upkeep costs could be made as low cost as Zappster.

  • Is Zappster Better Than Having 500 Satellite TV Channels?  Satellite footprints cover large geographic areas with a limited number of channels which must be shared by all households in the area. Thus, true video-on-demand controlled by the user is not possible with such systems. As with other video-on-demand services, satellite TV requires special equipment which cannot easily be moved as well as an in-home network to distribute content to players in different locations.

  • Are There Other Possible Uses Besides Video/Audio Rental For My Zappster Terminal?  YES! As storage densities increase and cost decreases, one could imagine using Personal Data Packs for storage of a video library. A 100GB pack could store 16 DVD movies or 133 VHS-quality movies. Family video could also be stored in Personal Data Packs and exchanged either directly or mediated by the Zappster Hybrid Distribution Network. Finally, since the Personal Data Pack is a disk storage unit, one could also imagine using it with a personal computer and using the Zappster network for software purchases, data delivery and other uses limited only by the imagination of users and software designers.

Investor/Developer Questions  

  • What Really Makes Zappster Technology Tick? The secret to Zappster is three-pronged. First, mass memory prices are decreasing rapidly even while density doubles every year. This makes for economical storage of large files. Second, a large portion of large file content is relatively static, thus users can often tolerate significant delay in receiving this content. When combined, these two factors suggest that far flung ubiquitous ultra high speed wireless or wireline networks are not necessary to deliver certain types of content. Thus, one can realize a tremendous cost savings by not needing to build out and support a massive high speed network for convenient large file delivery.

    A third item which may make Zappster even more irresistible in the future is the observation that ultra high wireless data rates can be obtained over short range wireless links. Thus, using inexpensive unlicensed bands such as the 5 GHz U-NII bands and inexpensive proprietary receiver/transmitter architectures, the Zappster user will be freed from the necessity of close physical contact with the Zappster Kiosk, and the possibilities of Kiosk drive-by and home delivery emerge as well.

  • What Is The Expected Cost Per Kiosk? $10000

  • What Is The Cost Per Download? We believe tens of cents per download is possible.

  • How Large A Network Is Needed? The size of the network can be small or large. A single Kiosk serving only 100 users per day and charging $3 per rental generates gross revenue of $9000/month. If operated like a typical busy video store, that figure could easily increase to greater than 1000 rentals per day at a single location. Likewise since the Kiosks do not REQUIRE special networking, the network cost scales with the single kiosk cost, or slightly below it with economy of scale.

  • Is Zappster Content Protected? Encrypted content can only be played from a Personal Data Pack by keying in an appropriate password. These passwords can be assigned individually by the network during download to allow single or multiple playback of content. Furthermore, the player can be designed to accept new keys provided by the network to play content previously downloaded to a Personal Data Pack or transferred between Personal Data Packs without an additional download. That is, the Personal Data Pack is an active device - as compared to a DVD disk or Video tape.

  • How Big Is The Zappster Market? Video rental today is a $20B business. Enron has teamed with Verizon, SBC and Blockbuster to provide an advanced video-on demand system. This partnership projects a $30B market in 8 years. Since the Zappster market includes mobile services as well as the more traditional in-home rental market, we expect at least a $30B market for Zappster services.

  • Who Are Your Competitors?

    Video-on-demand services, Satellite Distribution Networks, Video rental services, and retail entertainment outlets.

  • Can Multiple Entities Operate on a Single Zappster Network?  YES! Multiple encryption and validation databases could easily accommodate multiple providers. Kiosks could thus serve as shared, or even public repositories of information to be picked up and dropped off by different entities. A number of different providers could use the Zappster network independently within the confines of physical constraints on storage for large files and network speeds for labile content (voice-mail, e-mail, stock quotes, etc.) up/downloaded over the wireless/wireline network.

    See also Other Uses for Zappster

  • What Are The Zappster Revenue Streams?  Revenue can come from user subscriptions and one-time rental fees of any digital content. Of particular note, Zappster enables markets previously non-existent such as custom delivery of pre-release or rerun television network programming where video tape/DVD production and inventory costs would be prohibitive.

    Also, since the Personal Data Pack is an active device and the content can be altered or enhanced at the time of download, directed advertising is also a possible revenue stream. Furthermore, since we expect the mobile component of Zappster to increase video/audio consumption, the amount of advertising time may also be increased. Finally, one can imagine new types of interactive advertising where users are asked questions about advertisements or preferences and this information uploaded to the Kiosk during the next visit. Zappster thus enables greater targeted advertising revenues than traditional media and can combine market research with the process as well.

  • What Do You Want From Your Investors?
    • Support for an exploratory development effort (equipment, space and staff) which will result in a prototype system and a demonstration of Plug-in Zappster.
    • During development, we need access to content providers and a legal/business staff to procure content agreements as well as develop advertising revenue strategies.
    • With the technology and content agreements in place, we need funds to deploy a pilot study. These funds will be primarily for marketing and subsidizing the Personal Data Packs to achieve market penetration since the equipment cost is expected to be relatively low.
    • In parallel with the pilot we will require funds to develop Wireless Zappster technology, following a development course similar to Plug-in Zappster. Since this is state of the art wireless technology, this effort may require two years. However, it will be designed to function like Plug-In Zappster without the plug, so minimal (if any) higher level adjustments need be made.
    • After a successful pilot, we expect a full scale deployment and marketing effort to capture a large share of the entertainment rental market along with promising subsidiary markets which appear.
    • Funds for upgrade to Wireless Zappster both for customers and network operations.

  • How Long Will It Take To Deploy Zappster If You Had Everything You Needed? Development of Plug-in Zappster could be complete within a year. If marketing arrangements proceed in parallel, the pilot could begin during year 2 with full scale deployment by year 3. Wireless Zappster will proceed in parallel and be introduced by year 4 or 5.

  • What Do You Want For Yourselves? Each of the six Founders requires an equal non-dilutable percentage of equity and some reasonable per-download royalty in perpetuity in consideration for past work developing the Zappster concept. Additionally, at the discretion of the management team and under separate agreement, the various Founders may serve as consultants and/or employees of the corporate entity which develops Zappster. The Founders fully understand that the most rapid and successful development of Zappster will require skill sets beyond those of the Founders. They will therefore do whatever is necessary to bring Zappster rapidly and successfully to market, including at the discretion of management, stepping aside for personnel better equipped to handle what should be strong growth on a variety of fronts.
