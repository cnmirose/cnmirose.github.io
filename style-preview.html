Three lighter directions

Same content in each, so the only variable is the palette. Everything here is driven by one block of CSS custom properties per theme — picking one means swapping about twenty lines in site.css, not rebuilding pages.

Hover the thumbnails and the cards; the interactions differ in feel even though the markup is identical.
