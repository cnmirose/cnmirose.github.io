Better than Blockbuster?
•No store
•No employees
•No inventory
•Nothing ever out of stock
We see immediately that the PDP itself represents a new consumer product, but consider the rest of the comparison.  We have replaced a large store with a kiosk the size of an ATM, and costing perhaps $10,000.  It has no employees and no inventory– and nothing is ever out of stock.  That issue alone is a tremendous benefit– imagine knowing the movie you want will always be there.
The kiosk can be located in a retail store, a mall, or a bus terminal, on a footprint of a few square feet.  It will draw traffic, so it will be considered an attractive addition to any retail environment.  Because it costs so little, it can be made more frequently and conveniently available to the public.
But these advantages are just the beginning!
