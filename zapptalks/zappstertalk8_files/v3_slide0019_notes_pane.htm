In addition to this physical delivery channel, we envision that there will be a DSL-type Internet connection to most kiosks.  This will allow last-minute updates to information like news and financial reports, and can be used where special orders do not justify a physical visit.  One additional possibility is that personal information such as large email files and voicemail could be delivered to kiosks for pickup, or that customers could use the kiosk as a public access portal.
The entire process would be automated.  Customers would place orders– over the Internet for example, and the time of delivery would be automatically confirmed.  The content would be downloaded at the service center to the delivery disks and wireline connections automatically.
