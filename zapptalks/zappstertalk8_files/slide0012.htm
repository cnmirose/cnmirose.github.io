And of course, movies aren’t the only entertainment that can be downloaded in digital form.  The kiosk can offer a selection of CDs, and music in MP3 format for portable players.
Audio books are also large files that are becoming increasingly popular, and of course print formats would be trivial to handle as well. The Zappster kiosk can also deliver games.
We believe that network TV programming represents a big opportunity, and that Zappster can be a whole new distribution channel for network programming. You could download the evening’s entertainment at the train terminal, start to watch it on the train, and then finish at home.  In addition to current programs, you could download programs you’ve missed, and programs you saw a long time ago, and want to see again.  For the networks, this is an opportunity to pick up viewers they missed, and as an added incentive, it can be delivered with more valuable, targeted advertising.
