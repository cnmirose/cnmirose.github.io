Our content can go beyond entertainment as well, to news programming– not only TV and radio news, but newspapers and magazines.  Watch the news on the way home.  Get your local paper while traveling.
Microsoft would find our kiosk an excellent way to sell their software.
Finally, there’s a whole new area of information that truly conjures up the mobile internet– local information and advertising.  Detailed maps and directories, images of the local environment, advertising for stores and restaurants– even local history and walking tours, can be provided in the large files we can easily download.
The message here is that Zappster can begin as a competitor for conventional video rental, but it has the potential to be much more than that.  It allows any form of digital information to be made available at a very low delivery cost.
