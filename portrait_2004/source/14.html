The digital age leads to things like this FOR TRUE GNERDS ONLY: the scan rate of the CCD chip which comprises the imager for the camera leads to this sort of artifact. With a regular non-digital SLR camera, you'd actually have seen the prop blade frozen in place or smeared out. Here, the sequential nature of the CCD leads to time-delays between captured pixels and this somewhat cool artifact -- you could probably figure out the spin rate of the prop if you decided to be uber-gnerd.
