The Rose Family Dodges a Bullet

Saturday, September 18, 2010


Stephanie and Jason stopped at a N. Central Avenue Seven Eleven in White Plains on a bright sunny afternoon after a youth-group picnic.   Steph parked on the street outside while Jason went in for a gatorade.  He sensed something odd that made him uncomfy, so he quickly made his purchase and hopped back in the car.  Within seconds, police officers came out of nowhere with guns drawn -- not pointed down at the ready, but up in firing position.  Had Jason been IN the store as the police arrived, Stephanie WOULD have dashed in regardless -- with immeasurable FUBAR potential.

A Dad's job is to keep his family safe.  Such uncontrollable random things make for bolt upright night terrors, even when the bullet misses.
