In summary, we have discussed the difficulty of delivering giant files today.
We have shown that video rental is a effective technique, but that Zappster offers powerful advantages.  It is cheaper, serves a mobile population, and can deliver many new forms of content.
We have shown how magnetic storage technology is the key to a large menu of content in the kiosk, an almost infinite menu on special order through a hybrid network, and a small, low-cost PDP.
We think the costs look attractive.
Looking out a few more years, we think that Zappster can evolve into a dramatic new type of global network.
 What is particularly exciting is that we don’t need to invest huge sums for some speculative technology or some undeveloped market, capable of moving giant files anywhere, at low cost.
